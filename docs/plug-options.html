gbox Plug Options

Locally controlled plugs with energy monitoring, ranked for gbox. Claims are marked verified, sourced (seen in search results, page not opened) or unverified. Decisions added 2026-11-17.

Recommendation

Keep the Kasa HS110 on the SC-BOX. For a second metered plug before any new driver exists, buy a second legacy-firmware Kasa and decline every firmware update. Build the Shelly driver when you want a plug that is not Kasa.

sourced A 2020 TP-Link community thread lists the HS110 as End of Life on TP-Link's US site, with the KP115 named as its replacement. No 2026 listing was found, so whether a new HS110 can still be bought, and on what firmware, is unverified.

What gbox can drive today

verified gbox/plug.py has one driver, KasaLegacy: TP-Link's original protocol on TCP 9999, no login, with energy readings when the plug has a meter. discover() recognises KLAP plugs but cannot control them. Shelly, Tasmota and a generic HTTP driver are planned in docs/plan.md and none is built. Zigbee, Z-Wave and Matter plugs cannot be driven at all.

Test before relying on it: Kasa after an outage with no internet

sourced, untested here TP-Link support told a Tapo P110 owner that the plug syncs its clock from the internet, and after an outage with no internet its time-related features stop. A user described a "zombie mode": on and off work, energy monitoring is lost until the plug sees the internet. That was a Tapo. Whether an HS110 on the legacy protocol does the same is unverified.

It matters because the watchdog uses the meter to tell a powered-but-hung miner from one that never powered up, and the ISP drops within minutes of a grid loss. Test: block the router's WAN, power-cycle a bench plug, read get_sysinfo and emeter. Done when watts read after boot.
