Errors over three daysbad share, board resets and rejected shares, from the service log and the miner's own log

The strip sits under the existing three-day errors chart on the same time scale (the chart itself is left out of this prototype). One mark per rejected share at the time gbox read the miner's log, so a time is good to five minutes. Hover a mark for the detail. Two in the same read draw as one mark with the count.

Termsthe two entries this feature adds

stale share
a correct answer the pool refused because it arrived after the pool had moved to the next block. Pool timing, not the miner. A small share of them is normal; this miner's own rate over the last week is printed beside the count.
rejected share, not stale
a share the pool refused for any other reason, or one whose reason the miner's log did not say. Rare, and worth a look at the miner's log when it happens; it is marked X on the charts.
